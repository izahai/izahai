### 🔬 Research & Engineering Focus

I build and study intelligent systems at the intersection of **Generative Vision Models**, **Embodied AI / Robotics**, and **Interactive XR**:

- 🤖 **Humanoid Teleoperation & Motion Capture**: Full-body real-time teleoperation and retargeting at 50 Hz for the **Unitree G1 EDU (29 DoF)** humanoid robot using PICO 4 & ankle motion trackers ([**g1-pico-suspended-arm-mocap**](https://github.com/izahai/g1-pico-suspended-arm-mocap)).
- 🥽 **VR Locomotion & Scene-Aware Systems**: Real-time Inverse Kinematics (IK) avatar tracking with VIVE trackers in Unity ([**CycleWalkingVR**](https://github.com/izahai/CycleWalkingVR)) and context-aware 3D/VR outfit retrieval ([**SceneFit**](https://github.com/izahai/SceneFit) / [**SceneFit-Backend**](https://github.com/izahai/SceneFit-Backend)).
- 🧠 **Generative AI & Computer Vision**: Building diffusion pipelines from scratch ([**Anime-Face-Pytorch-Stable-Diffusion**](https://github.com/izahai/Anime-Face-Pytorch-Stable-Diffusion)), concept erasure & evaluation benchmarks ([**InfinityCE**](https://github.com/izahai/InfinityCE), [**CE-Eval**](https://github.com/izahai/CE-Eval)), and multi-label vision models ([**Multi-Label-Classification-with-MNIST-Digits**](https://github.com/izahai/Multi-Label-Classification-with-MNIST-Digits)).
- 🌐 **Core Systems & Graph Algorithms**: Reliable UDP (Go-Back-N + AIMD + SHA-256) hybrid file transfer protocol ([**hybrid-ftp**](https://github.com/izahai/hybrid-ftp)) and large-scale road network routing with Contraction Hierarchies & Transit Node Routing ([**Road Network Routing**](https://github.com/izahai/Contraction-Hierarchies-and-Transit-Node-Routing-in-Road-Networks)).
